# Revision of 21 September 2026

This revision adds a theory section, six measurement sections that read the quantities the
theory names, and the run records behind them. The earlier EMIT and OCO-2 results are
unchanged.

## Theory

`paper/sec_theory_new.tex` carries the statements: the conditional sensitivity criterion for
residual correction, a split-sample bias-variance identity, a finite-class selection bound,
the transfer of component error into retrieval error with constant one together with the
construction that attains it, the conditional error-profile theorem that fixes the rate, and
the same-data perturbation bounds for a learned-mean kernel predictor.

## Measurements

`paper/sec_results_new.tex` reports them in order:

- transmission-conditioned retrieval error, which separates the opaque bands from the rest;
- ten kernels on the six state inputs, one component at a time, with three combiners:
  Matern-1/2, -3/2, -5/2, Gaussian, a four-component spectral mixture, the NNGP and NTK
  arc-cosine kernels, a first-order additive Matern, and two relevance-determination fits;
- a regularisation suite for the network (control, weight-decay grid, early-stopping grid),
  each arm alone, with a residual kernel, and with a kernel on its last hidden layer;
- the ridge path and the perturbation bounds evaluated on held-out rows;
- a synthetic reference with a known error profile;
- the correction-coefficient corpus of the multi-fidelity benchmark rescored under its own
  protocol, in both of its configurations and on both of its splits;
- OCO-2 rerun with the selection carried out through the Gram matrix of the design.

The kernel arithmetic in `multikernel_gpu.py` is written in place so that a 15 GB accelerator
holds one block of the float64 Gram matrix at n = 18,884: the arc-cosine recursion scales rows
and columns instead of forming the outer product of the diagonals, the additive polynomial is
accumulated with `addcmul_`, and each dimension's temporary is released before the next is
allocated.

## Records

`results/e2bc` holds the multi-kernel lanes (four components by three seeds) and the
regularisation suite; `results/oco2`, `results/pkanrtm`, `results/ridge` and `results/tc` hold
the OCO-2 campaign, the benchmark rescoring, the ridge path and the transmission-conditioned
diagnostics. Each file records the selected hyperparameters, the split sizes and the metrics
on the test rows.
