# Withdrawal of Addendum 2 (PREREGISTRATION_TQ_ADDENDUM_2.md, sha256 5cf05146c8be069f..)

Written 2026-09-23 06:4xZ, before any lane of seed 105 has started on either machine.

Addendum 2 moved seed 105 from the DGX to Kaggle on the strength of estimated lane times. The measured times do not
support it. On the DGX a width-2000 lane took 123 minutes (admissible, seed 101) and 159 minutes (raw, seed 101),
not four hours, against 91 minutes for the one finished Kaggle width-2000 lane (admissible, seed 106); the width-512
lanes take about 100 to 113 minutes on both machines; and two Kaggle lanes pushed at 04:34Z were still running more
than two hours later. With these times the move would lengthen the campaign rather than shorten it.

Addendum 2 is therefore withdrawn before it took effect. Seed 105 runs on the DGX, as in Addendum 1, and its six
queue lines are restored in their original places. Nothing else changes: seeds 101-105 run on the DGX and seeds
106-110 on Kaggle, all six lanes of a seed on one kind of machine.
